Buy an item — it goes straight to storage. Place it from the quick bar (bottom center) or its expanded view. Grouped the same way here. Stock is limited and refreshes on a timer, so not everything is always available.

Your restaurant's overall progression — driven purely by lifetime revenue, 100 levels deep. This is where appliances, recipes, and furniture actually unlock (not the Achievements tab, which is just cash bonuses). Some levels pay out money or free ingredients instead of unlocking anything.

Enabled recipes can be ordered by customers. Customers always try to order the priciest recipe whose ingredients are all in stock, falling back to cheaper ones — plain rice never runs out. Cooking is free, customers only pay the price. Combo dishes need two or more ingredients but sell for much more.

Controls

  • There's no character to move — you manage the restaurant entirely from the menus and the build cursor. Everything runs on hired staff.
  • E — open/close this menu
  • ESC — put down whatever you're currently holding
  • Left-click a tile to place whatever you're holding; right-click a placed object to Move, Store, or Sell it
  • Hold the right mouse button and drag to pan the camera around the map
  • The quick bar (bottom center) holds both storage and hired-but-unplaced staff — click a slot to pick it up; click ▲ to see everything, grouped by category
  • If a slot has more than one item (or staff member), placing one keeps the next in hand automatically — keep clicking tiles to place the whole stack, or press ESC to stop early

Getting started

  • You start on an empty plot with nothing built yet — a free starting kit is waiting in the quick bar (bottom center) instead: a Chef, a Waiter, a Fridge, a Stove, a Sink, an Order Stand, 30 Walls, 4 Window Walls, a Door, a Table, and 2 Chairs. Click a slot to pick it up, then click tiles to build your own room — buy more from the Shop tab once you outgrow the kit
  • No customers will show up until a Door is placed somewhere — that's the only entrance they path through
  • Hired staff (including your starting Chef and Waiter) wait in the quick bar until placed — click their slot there, then click an open tile to put them to work; they'll keep refilling into your hand if there's more than one
  • Customers pay automatically the moment they finish eating — watch for the little gold "+$" that pops up over their head
  • Waiters take orders, deliver food, and bus dirty tables; chefs fetch ingredients from the Fridge and cook them at a Stove — it all happens on its own once they're placed, as long as the ingredient's in stock
  • A Counter works as bar seating too — put a chair next to one and customers will sit and eat right there, no table needed
  • Grow crops (wheat, tomato, cabbage, corn, potato, carrot, onion, pumpkin) on their matching plot — a Farmer plants and harvests them automatically; any Fridge shows your current stock. Water tops itself up automatically from any placed Sink
  • Fishermen catch fish near water on their own — usually shrimp, sometimes a rarer salmon — and freeze it at a Freezer, no extra prep needed
  • Ranchers feed Chickens and Cows from their matching feeder (stocked with wheat from a fridge) and process grown animals at the Animal Shack
  • Train staff from the Staff tab to level them up — faster, more capable versions of the same role
  • Watch the 📋 Orders panel (top of screen) to see what's cooking and what's ready to deliver
