Controls

  • WASD — move around
  • SPACE — interact (serve a table, cook, collect payment...)
  • E — open/close this menu
  • ESC — put down whatever you're currently holding
  • Right-click a placed object to Move, Store, or Sell it — appliances work from any side, just walk up and press SPACE
  • The quick bar (bottom center) is your storage — click a slot to start placing that item; click ▲ to see everything, grouped by category
  • If a slot has more than one item, placing one keeps the next in hand automatically — keep clicking tiles to place the whole stack, or press ESC to stop early

Getting started

  • Everything starts in storage — place your Fridge, Stove, Sink, Order Stand, Paying Booth, Table and Chairs anywhere on the field to build your kitchen
  • Interact with a seated customer's table to take their order, then cook it: grab the ingredient at the Fridge, cook it at the Stove, and drop the finished dish at the Order Stand
  • A Counter works as bar seating too — put a chair next to one and customers will sit and eat right there, no table needed
  • Grow crops (wheat, tomato, cabbage, corn, potato, carrot, onion, pumpkin) on their matching plot, wait for them to grow, then harvest — any Fridge shows your current stock, and staff will always use whichever fridge is closest to them
  • Fishermen catch fish near water on their own — usually shrimp, sometimes a rarer salmon — and freeze it at a Freezer, no extra prep needed
  • Ranchers feed Chickens and Cows from their matching feeder (stocked with wheat from a fridge) and process grown animals at the Animal Shack
  • Hire staff from the Staff tab to automate cooking, serving, cleaning and farming (farmers plant and harvest on their own) — train them to level up
  • Watch the 📋 Orders panel (top of screen) to see what's cooking and what's ready to deliver
