Controls

  • WASD — move around
  • SPACE — interact (serve a table, cook, collect payment...)
  • E — open/close this menu
  • ESC — put down whatever you're currently holding
  • Right-click a placed object to Move, Rotate, Store, or Sell it

Getting started

  • Everything starts in Storage — place your Fridge, Stove, Sink, Order Stand, Paying Booth, Table and Chairs anywhere on the field to build your kitchen
  • Interact with a seated customer's table to take their order, then cook it: grab the ingredient at the Fridge, cook it at the Stove, and drop the finished dish at the Order Stand
  • Some recipes (like Shrimp) must visit the Prep Station before the fridge
  • Plant wheat on a Farm Plot, wait for it to grow, then harvest it — the fridge shows your current wheat stock
  • Hire staff from the Staff tab to automate cooking, serving, cleaning and farming — train them to level up
  • Watch the 📋 Orders panel (top of screen) to see what's cooking and what's ready to deliver
